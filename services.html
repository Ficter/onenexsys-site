Questions

Before You Get in Touch

Straight answers to the questions we hear most.

Do you work with small offices?

Yes. Our packages are designed for offices of roughly 5 to 40 people. Larger or multi-site work is scoped separately.

Can I get a price before we start?

Yes. Every project is quoted in writing with a fixed scope. Managed support is priced per user per month once we have seen your setup.

Are there government grants for this?

Schemes such as Enterprise Singapore's EDGE Grant and CSA's programmes can help SMEs fund digital and cybersecurity upgrades. Eligibility depends on your company and the solution, so check current terms before you commit.

What do I get at the end of a project?

A written handover: asset list, configurations, cable and rack labelling, admin access records and what we changed, so you are never locked in to us.

Can you work alongside my current IT provider?

Yes. We can start with an audit and either work alongside them on a specific project or take over day-to-day support.
