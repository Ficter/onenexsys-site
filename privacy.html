Privacy Policy
Your Data, Handled With Care

How ONENEXSYS PTE. LTD. collects, uses and protects personal data. Last updated 21 November 2026.

ONENEXSYS PTE. LTD. ("we") handles personal data in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

What we collect

When you use our contact form we collect your name, email address, company name (optional), phone number (optional), the service you are interested in and your message. If you use the Infrastructure Planner and choose to send your result to us, your answers are added to your message. The planner itself runs in your browser and keeps your result only in your browser session until you send it.

Why we collect it

Only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and, if you become a client, deliver and support our services. We do not sell your data or use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Who handles it

  • Our team, who read the enquiries sent to contact@onenexsys.com.
  • Resend, the email delivery service that sends your enquiry to us.
  • Vercel, which hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the website typeface and therefore receives your IP address when a page loads.

Some of these providers process data outside Singapore. We expect them to protect it to a standard comparable to the PDPA.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept for as long as needed for the engagement and for legal or accounting requirements.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set tracking cookies. If we add analytics in future we will update this page.

Your rights

You may ask to access or correct the personal data we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by emailing contact@onenexsys.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Contact

Data protection enquiries: contact@onenexsys.com.
